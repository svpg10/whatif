AI Simulation Disclaimer

Updated 20 October 2026.

Illustrations, not forecasts

WhatIf explores decisions using simplified, deterministic rules for built-in scenarios and Google Gemini-generated strategies and assumptions for custom questions. It does not access live research or verify your question against external facts.

How to read results

Custom scenario scores are illustrative 0–100 indices, with higher values meaning better outcomes under the stated assumptions. They are not probabilities, confidence intervals or measured evidence. Custom controls adjust those indices using fixed teaching heuristics; the interface explains the direction of each adjustment.

Built-in scenario metrics also use simplified teaching heuristics rather than calibrated empirical models. Trajectories use a 90-day illustrative window even when implementation may take months or years. A leading branch reflects your chosen weights and these assumptions, not an objectively correct decision.

AI limitations

AI-generated strategies can misunderstand context, omit stakeholders, introduce bias, or make unsupported assumptions. Review and edit the controls before comparing strategies. If generation fails, the app reports the failure instead of substituting a built-in example.

Use your own judgement

Outputs are not professional financial, medical, legal or safety advice. Verify material claims independently and consult qualified professionals for consequential decisions. Avoid entering sensitive information into custom questions.
