WhatIf is an AI-powered decision simulation platform that lets users explore hypothetical scenarios through structured, rule-based modelling and AI-generated narrative outputs. It is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME].

We do not collect your name, email address, payment information, or account credentials unless you explicitly provide them through a separate process.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Process your simulation request and deliver the WhatIf experience

  • Debug errors and diagnose technical issues
  • Detect and prevent abuse or misuse of the platform
  • Respond to support requests, if any are submitted
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

We do not use your simulation prompts for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

WhatIf does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

WhatIf uses browser session storage to:

  • Remember whether you have completed the introduction animation in your current session
  • Store your current simulation state (question, variable values, results) so the application functions correctly as you navigate between pages
  • Record your consent decision for the current session

This session storage data is automatically removed when you close your browser tab or window. It is not transmitted to any server by WhatIf itself (though your browser or operating system may handle storage differently).

If WhatIf is served over a CDN or hosting platform, that infrastructure may set its own cookies. Please refer to the relevant hosting provider's cookie policy.
